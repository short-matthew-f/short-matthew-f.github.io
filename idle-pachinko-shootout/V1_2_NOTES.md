# v1.2 Playtest Notes

- Preserve the roughly four-minute first unupgraded death cadence observed in v1.1.
- Death upgrade flow must always return to the Ride Again decision after closing the upgrade sheet.
- Blueprints are availability gates, not automatic unlocks. Clear milestones: 5 / 10 / 15 / 22 / 30 / 40. Buying a blueprint unlocks permission to separately purchase the related peg/ammo.
- Enemies move continuously and independently in visual lanes. Ghouls are fast, Zombies medium, Trolls slow/heavy, Ghosts ranged and hold back.
- Tap an enemy to focus fire. Focus persists until changed or the target dies; multi-target effects radiate from that primary target.
- Adaptive DPS-based difficulty remains disabled.
