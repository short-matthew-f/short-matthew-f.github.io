What is happening?

The fold-and-cut theorem says any straight-line shape can be made by folding one sheet of paper flat and making one complete straight cut.

The theorem solver is authoritative for the crease pattern and exact flat-folded vertex positions. The app uses solver corridor data when it can prove a simple packet sequence, otherwise it animates the construction as a coordinated compound fold.

When the solver can validate a packet-by-packet sequence, the app shows those folds separately. Otherwise the general compound fold is staged as a parent-to-child cascade so fewer parts move at once before the paper settles onto the solver’s exact flat state.

You can draw by tapping, or enter exact point coordinates as a secondary input mode.

Geometry foundation: Danielle Wang’s MIT-licensed 2017 Fold & Cut implementation for MIT 6.849, based on the straight-skeleton method of Demaine, Demaine & Lubiw.

Enter coordinates

One point per line, in order around the shape. Use x, y; negative values and decimals are fine. The last point connects back to the first automatically.
