# Lane Warden P2-0.19.0 — Broken Switchback

Broken Switchback is the third battle on the short Act 1 route. Alternating fall lines telegraph for seven seconds before a collapse.

- Settled Warden Presence braces the lane, grants the strongest tempo, and blunts the surge.
- A completed Tower Emplacement, Muster Camp, or Field Relay can hold the lane while the Warden acts elsewhere, with a smaller tempo reward.
- An unprotected fall line damages the Bastion and its deployed tower while sharply increasing local enemy pressure.
- Battle rules, deployment capacity, rewards, saves, and cohort simulation remain unchanged outside this encounter profile.

The encounter advances to the Ashen Rift after victory or a payable normal Last Stand.
