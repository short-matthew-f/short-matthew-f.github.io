# P1-0.12.0 — Conscript Clarity + Outer Approach Balance

**Date:** 2026-08-23  
**Status:** ACTIVE HUMAN TUNING ITERATION  
**Design baseline:** 1.7

## Sequence

This release follows the requested order:

1. repair/clarify Conscript state;
2. then tune the first production battle.

The lane-card control model from P1-0.11.2 is retained.

## Conscript contract

Conscript is usable when:

- the battle is active;
- the Warden is not reforming;
- the Warden is physically settled in a lane rather than walking;
- the cooldown is zero;
- the current lane has committed units.

A legal P1 deployment already requires units in both lanes, so the player-facing rule is effectively: **Warden settled in a lane + cooldown ready**.

On use, Conscript reinforces that lane's next two pulses. UI now reports:

- `NEXT 2 PULSES` before use;
- cooldown plus `2 PULSES QUEUED` / `1 PULSE QUEUED` after use;
- `REINFORCED NOW` while a reinforced pulse is active;
- `MOVING`, `REFORM`, or other explicit ineligibility instead of false READY.

Lane cards also surface `REINF ×2`, `REINF ×1`, or `REINFORCED`.

## Outer Approach tuning profile

P1-0.11.x demonstrated that the first battle demanded near-perfect ability cadence to make offensive progress. That is inappropriate for the Act 1 teaching battle.

P1-0.12.0 changes new battles as follows:

- allied base pressure: **×1.05**;
- enemy base pressure: **×0.90**;
- Guard regeneration: **×0.60**;
- starting Guard health: **90** instead of 100;
- each lane front begins **+0.03** farther forward;
- Sunder front shove: **0.050** total instead of 0.035;
- Sunder Guard burst: **24** total instead of 18;
- Sunder exposed-Gate burst: **18** total instead of 14;
- Reclamation trigger: **3:30** instead of 3:00.

No change in this pass to:

- unit costs or deployment budgets;
- tower costs;
- battle purchase costs;
- Rally duration/cooldown;
- Waypoint cooldown;
- Conscript's two-pulse quantity or 18s cooldown;
- Reclamation artillery cadence, telegraph, or Guard rebuild cadence;
- Last Stand rules.

## Intended feel

A player who commits the Warden and abilities to a siege lane should be able to make visible progress and break one Guard well before Reclamation. A slower but engaged player should still have a plausible route to the Gate. Ignoring the battle should still lose through the neglected lane.

This is a human tuning iteration, not a frozen production balance claim.
