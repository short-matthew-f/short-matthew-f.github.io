# Lane Warden — P1-0.11.1 UI Cleanup

**Build:** P1-0.11.1  
**Scope:** presentation/control iteration only  
**Balance base:** P1-0.11.0 unchanged

## Why this patch exists

Human P1 play exposed two separate problems that must be isolated:

1. the battle is presently extremely difficult to make progress in;
2. the battle controls are also harder to parse than they should be.

P1-0.11.1 changes only the second category so the next human run can distinguish interface friction from encounter balance.

## Changes

### Ability rail

- Ready abilities receive a teal/green ready treatment plus the explicit word `READY`.
- Cooldown abilities receive a red treatment plus the remaining cooldown in seconds.
- Commander-incapacitated abilities show `DOWN`.
- A cooldown becoming ready receives one short readiness pulse.

Color is not the sole carrier; state text is always present.

### Lane-local actions

The detached lower-left structure-control button is retired from presentation.

Tapping a bottom lane card now opens a compact tray anchored directly above that lane card containing:

- Fortify Tower;
- Overcharge;
- Reinforce 2 Pulses.

The tray mirrors the existing P1 purchase rules without changing costs or effects.

Dismissal:

- tap the same lane card again;
- tap the tray close control;
- tap outside the lane card/tray.

### Commander movement

Lane cards expose a direct `WALK HERE` affordance. The lane holding the Commander says `COMMANDER`; an active transfer says `MOVING`.

Waypoint no longer presents the old target menu. Activating Waypoint turns the lane cards into `WAYPOINT HERE` targets. Tap a lane to relocate; tap Waypoint again or tap outside to cancel.

### Tactical reference

The former `ACTIONS` surface is now a `?` tactical-reference control.

- It remains protected reading.
- It explains abilities rather than executing them.
- Normal walking is no longer buried in this reference panel.
- Right-rail abilities remain the action surface.
- Tap outside closes it.

## Explicit non-changes

P1-0.11.1 does **not** change:

- Guard health or regeneration;
- friendly/enemy pressure;
- unit/tower stats;
- battle-gold income;
- purchase prices/effects;
- Commander ability power/cooldowns;
- Reclamation trigger (3:00) or Reclamation rules;
- Core/Gate health;
- deployment budgets.

Those belong to the next balance iteration after UI feedback.
