# Lane Warden — P2-0.15.0 Living Lanes

**Build:** P2-0.15.0  
**Stable install scope:** `/lol-the-spire/lane-warden-prod-v0.10.4/`  
**Purpose:** Replace the aggregate-front battlefield with a spatial cohort simulation without discarding the P1/P2 combat, reward, or encounter work.

## What changed

The old `lane.front` value is no longer the authoritative combat model. It remains only as a derived compatibility/readout value for legacy HUD and encounter systems.

Each lane now contains persistent friendly and enemy cohorts with position, health, movement speed, combat power, and engagement state.

- initial friendly and enemy cohorts enter the field at separate positions;
- allied pulses spawn new friendly cohorts at the rear;
- enemy waves spawn independently from the Gate side;
- cohorts move until an opposing cohort is in engagement range;
- later cohorts can catch up and support an existing engagement;
- engaged groups stop rather than passing through one another;
- when an engagement is won, surviving cohorts resume marching;
- friendly breakthroughs damage the Guard and then the exposed Gate;
- enemy breakthroughs damage the Bastion and then the shared Core;
- multiple engagements may coexist in one lane.

## Commander

The Warden now has an actual lane position.

- tapping a point on either lane orders the Warden to that position;
- same-lane movement occurs continuously through lane space;
- cross-lane movement still uses the junction and respects existing movement modifiers;
- Waypoint teleports the Warden to the active friendly line in the target lane;
- Presence is a radius around the Warden rather than a whole-lane multiplier;
- Rally is centered on the Warden's current position;
- Sunder hits a nearby enemy cohort first, or a Guard/Gate when the Warden is physically in objective range.

## Towers

Tower placement now has actual geography.

- forward and rear slots occupy different lane positions;
- Bolt/defense towers attack local single targets;
- Scatter/swarm towers damage clustered local cohorts;
- Frost/slow towers slow enemies crossing their zone;
- War Pylon/amplify towers buff friendly cohorts crossing their zone.

Existing reward towers retain their reward hooks and inherit the closest living-lane tower behavior.

## Reward integration retained

P2-0.13.0 reward progression remains authoritative. Living Lanes preserves or translates the currently implemented run effects, including:

- Wardrunner / Junction Beacon movement reductions;
- Rally Standard and Deep Conscript pulse behavior;
- spatial Sunder Echo and Breach Sapper bonus damage;
- Siege Ram Hooked Yoke Gate interaction;
- Pulse Prism reinforced cohorts;
- Gatebite Sigil on a living-lane Guard break;
- Bastion Oath, Miser's Cog, Reclamation Clock, capacity, and reward-control relics;
- Pulse Drummer and Slingline Cadence spawn-position bonuses;
- tower upgrades and battle-gold purchases.

## Split Causeway

P2-0.14.0's raid/rotation profile is retained and now wraps the living simulation. Its temporary enemy-power changes affect actual cohorts and enemy waves rather than an aggregate front-pressure coefficient.

## Interaction/UI

The production battle canvas now renders the living simulation directly:

- visible cohorts march along both lanes;
- cohort health is visible;
- active/supporting engagements are visually marked;
- objectives and tower zones are spatially visible;
- the Warden's Presence radius is visible;
- the complete lane battlefield fits in the battle view rather than requiring the old abstract-front camera model;
- tap-to-move replaces battlefield drag/pan as the primary map interaction.

## Intentional limitations of this milestone

This is the first production Living Lanes milestone, not the final tactical simulation.

Not yet implemented as separate battlefield entities:

- individual units inside a cohort;
- ranged projectile simulation / explicit firing lines;
- unit-specific collision sizes and formations;
- retreat, morale, or rout behavior;
- support units choosing independent follow targets;
- player-issued cohort movement orders;
- enemy named unit types beyond the initial line/raider/brute wave archetypes.

The next design decision should be based on human play: whether simply watching cohorts arrive, collide, reinforce, and break through makes the battlefield itself interesting enough to deserve further spatial depth.
