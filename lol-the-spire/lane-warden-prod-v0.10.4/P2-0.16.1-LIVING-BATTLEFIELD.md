# Lane Warden P2-0.16.1 — Living Battlefield

P2-0.16.1 is a battlefield-feel patch on the P2-0.16.0 Tactical Models release. It does not change cohort combat, rewards, progression, encounter tuning, or save schema.

## Organic battlefield

- North and South now follow separate curving road profiles.
- Procedural ground patches, grass, scrub, rocks, camps, route stones, and a creek fill the dead field without requiring texture downloads or runtime network dependencies.
- Objectives, towers, cohorts, attack effects, and the Commander sit on and follow the curved road geometry.

## Cross-lane movement

- Old Gate Steps, Dry Creek Ford, and Ridge Cut replace the single center bar.
- Cross-lane orders choose the shortest cut-through from the Commander’s current position.
- Mid-route retargeting keeps the active crossing, allowing natural reversal rather than teleporting to a different route.
- Cross-lane distance now has enough temporal weight to be visible and tactically understandable.

## Camera and weight

- Pinch zoom scales the whole battlefield in both axes, including models and terrain, from 68% to 225%.
- The pinch midpoint stays anchored and the zoomed field can be panned horizontally and vertically.
- Cohort presentation uses visual damping while simulation positions remain authoritative.
- March and engagement motion has lower amplitude and slower cadence; formations align to the local road tangent.
- Close zoom exposes individual miniature clashes without changing the fixed three-quarter tactical camera.

## Compatibility

- Save key and schema are unchanged.
- Stable install scope remains `/lol-the-spire/lane-warden-prod-v0.10.4/`.
- P2-0.16.0 model sources, atlas, manifest, and licensing records remain authoritative.
- `build.json` remains the final production write.
