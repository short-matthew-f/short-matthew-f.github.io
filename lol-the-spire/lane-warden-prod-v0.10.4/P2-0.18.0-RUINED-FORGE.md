# Lane Warden P2-0.18.0 — Ruined Forge

The Ruined Forge is the first production build-shaping node. It presents three deterministic, legal projects targeting units or towers already owned. One project may be installed for Salvage; the decision saves immediately, is irreversible for the run, and advances to the next battle seam.

The Forge reuses the authoritative reward upgrade catalog and the already-live battle effects. It does not introduce a second upgrade inventory or change reward packets, battle economy, deployment capacity, or encounter balance.

This release also expands the Living Lanes world height from 138% to 182% of the phone viewport. The overview zoom is 0.68, lanes sit at 31% and 69% of world height, and the full two-dimensional camera can pan through the larger upper and lower shoulders. Worksites remain reachable and visible at overview scale; pinch inspection continues to scale models in both axes.

Deployment remains in `lane-warden-prod-v0.10.4/`. Payload files are published before `build.json`.
