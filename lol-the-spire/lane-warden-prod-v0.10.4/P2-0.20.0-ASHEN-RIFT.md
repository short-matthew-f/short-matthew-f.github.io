# Lane Warden P2-0.20.0 — Ashen Rift

The Ashen Rift is an irreversible, immediately saved run decision between the Broken Switchback and the Ironbound Gate.

- Offer one Ember to reveal and install one deterministic hidden boon: faster Warden routes, wider Presence, or faster fieldwork construction.
- Or strip the Rift for 160 Salvage and preserve the run's Ember failure budget.
- The offer is seeded by the run, so reloads cannot reroll it.
- The decision adds no combat entity and does not alter reward packets or save identity.

Both choices advance to the Ironbound Gate.
