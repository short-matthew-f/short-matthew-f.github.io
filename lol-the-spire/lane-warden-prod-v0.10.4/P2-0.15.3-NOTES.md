# Lane Warden P2-0.15.3 — Commander Movement Normalization

## Why this patch exists
P2-0.15.2 restored mid-move retargeting and battlefield camera controls, but cross-lane travel still used the legacy presentation split of 45% of travel time to reach the junction, 10% to cross it, and 45% to leave it. Because those percentages ignored actual segment length, the Warden visibly changed speed during a single trip and different trips could feel arbitrarily fast or slow.

## New movement rule
The Warden now has one spatial movement speed.

- Same-lane duration is proportional to horizontal distance.
- Cross-lane routes are measured as: current point → junction → vertical crossing → target point.
- Every segment advances at the same battlefield-space speed.
- Short routes take less time than long routes.
- Mid-route retargeting rebuilds the route from the Warden's actual current position.
- Reversing direction follows the same rule; there is no artificial restart or fixed-duration lane swap.

## Movement rewards
Movement modifiers now multiply travel speed consistently instead of distorting individual segments:

- Junction Spurs
- Wardrunners
- Junction Beacon

These effects can stack as before.

## Compatibility
The old `commander.walkTime` remains in the legacy data schema for compatibility, but Living Lanes no longer treats it as a universal duration for every lane change.

## Unchanged
No combat damage, cohort cadence, reward probabilities, reward values, Reclamation timing, or encounter tuning changed in this patch.
