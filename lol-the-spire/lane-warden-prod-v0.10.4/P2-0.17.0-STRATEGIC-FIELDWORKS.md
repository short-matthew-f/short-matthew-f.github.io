# Lane Warden P2-0.17.0 — Strategic Fieldworks

P2-0.17.0 adds a first non-combat Commander commitment system to the Living Battlefield. It preserves the existing stable install scope, save key, deployment budgets, encounter rules, and reward architecture.

## Strategic worksites

- Two fixed sites sit beyond each lane shoulder: one rear and one forward.
- Tapping a site routes the Warden there through the existing spatial movement model and a short visible site approach.
- A Tower Emplacement costs 32 gold and 8 seconds, then supplies autonomous local fire.
- A Muster Camp costs 38 gold and 9 seconds, then relocates scheduled friendly cohort arrivals while the site remains controlled. It does not create free cohorts.
- A Field Relay costs 28 gold and 7 seconds, then shortens travel through its geographically paired cut-through by 22%.
- Construction suspends Commander Presence. Leaving pauses work without refunding the committed plan or discarding progress.
- One plan permanently occupies each site for that battle.

## Battlefield space

- The tactical world is 38% taller.
- Worksite clearings and curved spur roads occupy the terrain above North and below South.
- The default phone overview still includes both lanes and their outer shoulders.
- Existing two-axis pan, pinch inspection, tap-to-move, recenter, lane focus, cut-through selection, and mid-route retargeting remain active.

## Breach flow

- Destroyed Guards no longer retain an invisible movement cap.
- Friendly cohorts pass the breach and compress into multiple assault files near the Gate.
- Rebuilt Guards do not teleport cohorts that already crossed the original threshold backward.

## Compatibility

Fieldworks are optional battle state initialized lazily for old saves. Deployment legality, pulse totals, combat coefficients, Commander abilities, Reclamation, Last Stand, reward packets, progression, and the run save key are unchanged.
