# Lane Warden P2-0.21.0 — Ironbound Gate

The Ironbound Gate completes the current short Act 1 route with a distinct Gatekeeper battle.

- The Gatekeeper appears as a large battlefield commander between the two Gates.
- Repeating Iron Edicts target the most advanced friendly lane after a six-second warning.
- Settled Warden Presence breaks an Edict most effectively. Completed fieldworks can absorb it while preserving Commander freedom.
- Missing an Edict damages the lane's Bastion and tower and amplifies enemy pressure. Breaking one Guard enrages the surviving Guard lane.
- Normal victory grants 260 Salvage and a two-component premium packet: three Relic offers and three Act Growth offers.
- Gatekeeper Last Stand costs two Embers. It opens the road but ends the act without the premium packet; insufficient Embers make the result terminal.

Payload files, release notes, service-worker assets, and shell identifiers are published before `build.json` advertises P2-0.21.0.
