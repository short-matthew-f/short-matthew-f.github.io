# Lane Warden — P2-0.14.0

## Split Causeway — Easy Pool rotation encounter

P2-0.14.0 converts Split Causeway from a reward-effects proving ground into the second authored Act 1 Easy-Pool battle.

### Tactical identity

Outer Approach remains the concentration/breach baseline. Split Causeway instead tests Commander rotation and lane triage.

- raids alternate between South and North;
- each raid gives an 8-second telegraph;
- baseline Commander walk time remains 7 seconds, so a prompt reaction can reach the lane without Waypoint;
- being settled in the signaled lane when the raid hits braces/intercepts it;
- successful intercepts substantially blunt the surge, shove that lane's front +0.020, restore 0.5s Read reserve, and grant +8 battle gold;
- missing the intercept deals 11 Bastion damage and 4 tower HP immediately, then produces a stronger 8-second enemy-pressure surge;
- after each surge there is a 9-second rotation window and the next lane is forecast;
- Split Causeway starts with both Bastions at 84 and Guards at 82;
- normal enemy pressure between raids is intentionally lighter than Outer Approach so the encounter has a clear quiet/raid rhythm;
- Reclamation begins at 3:45.

### Reward interaction goals

The encounter is intended to make existing P2 rewards legible rather than introduce a separate progression system. Movement and Commander rewards should be especially noticeable, while Unit/Tower/Capacity rewards still change how safely each lane can be left unattended.

### UI

A compact causeway bar appears only during Split Causeway and shows:

- next-lane forecast during rotation windows;
- RAID SIGNAL + countdown during telegraph;
- BRACED if the Commander is already settled in the target lane;
- INTERCEPTED or SURGE after impact.

The lane cards also expose NEXT, RAID, BRACED, INTERCEPTED, or SURGE states. The layer is designed for the existing mobile-first landscape battle layout rather than replacing it.

### Regression

The encounter patch extends the internal self-test with:

- Split Causeway profile creation;
- telegraph >= baseline walk time;
- successful intercept path;
- missed-intercept damage path.

Outer Approach balance values are not changed by this release.
