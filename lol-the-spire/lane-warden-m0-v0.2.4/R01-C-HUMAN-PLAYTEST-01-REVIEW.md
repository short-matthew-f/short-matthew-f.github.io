# R01-C Human Playtest 01 — Review

**Source:** `R01-C-human-playtest-01.json`  
**Build:** M0-0.2.3 · R01-C · Siege + delay · 1×  
**Disposition:** **Strong exploratory pacing/consequence evidence; retain R01-C for the next UX isolation.**

## Result

- Win at **265.0s (4:25)**.
- North Guard broke at **174.41s**.
- DL-001 fork opened at **160s**.
- South Bastion finished at **0.6% HP**; it did not break before the Gate.
- Core remained untouched because the Gate fell just before Bastion collapse.
- Interventions: **2 Pushes + 1 Overdrive**, **180g spent**, 158g earned.
- Debrief: **sacrifice=yes / Guard read=clear / gold fork=yes**.

## Interpretation

This is a better decision result than the raw duration alone implies. The player used the starting Push at 5.5s, pushed North again at 110.3s, saw the fork at 160s, broke North at 174.4s, then had another 60g decision at 209s and spent it offensively on North rather than stabilizing South. The Gate fell with the sacrifice lane within roughly one percent of Bastion collapse.

That is a successful commitment/sacrifice race under informed play. It is also an aggressive intervention-heavy line, so its 4:25 duration should not by itself trigger another global durability increase. R01-C remains provisional rather than shipping balance, but it is frozen for ATT-001 so the next change can isolate attention signaling.

## What this does not prove

- A fresh player will discover the fork without coaching.
- Every viable strategy falls inside the 5–10 minute ordinary-battle range.
- The danger-attention layer is needed or correctly tuned.

Those remain separate questions.
