What is happening?

The fold-and-cut theorem says any straight-line shape can be made by folding one sheet of paper flat and making one complete straight cut.

V2 is sequence-first. For a convex polygon with reflectional symmetry, it first attempts an explicit all-layers simple-fold construction: symmetry fold, successive end-edge halving, angle-bisector folds, and final paper tucks.

If that explicit construction cannot be validated cleanly, V2 falls back to the general straight-skeleton theorem solver and its staged compound fold. It does not invent a simple sequence for shapes that the secondary solver cannot support.

You can draw by tapping or enter exact coordinates, one x, y pair per line.

Geometry foundation: Danielle Wang’s MIT-licensed 2017 Fold & Cut implementation for MIT 6.849, based on the straight-skeleton method of Demaine, Demaine & Lubiw.
